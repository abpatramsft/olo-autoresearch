# Notice

Olo is an independently written project-local prototype inspired by the
autoresearch process and product ideas demonstrated by
[evo-hq/evo](https://github.com/evo-hq/evo), including experiment trees,
worktree isolation, gates, shared traces, specialist agents, stall-driven
iteration, and a local dashboard.

No Evo source files or visual assets are required at runtime.
